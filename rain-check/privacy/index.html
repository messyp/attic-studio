Support Privacy policy

In short

Rain Check has no account and we don't collect your personal data ourselves. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some information to show and measure those ads, as described below. Rain Check+ removes the banner and the ads between levels.

What stays on your iPhone

Your levels, streaks, Garden Diary, hearts, acorns, fireflies and settings are kept only on your iPhone. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To show ads, Google's AdMob service may collect your device's advertising identifier, your IP address and an approximate location worked out from it, the type of device and software you use, and how you interact with the ads. Google uses this to choose, show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. If you're in the UK or the European Economic Area, the game asks for your consent before ads can be personalised. On iPhone, the advertising identifier is only shared if you allow tracking when Apple's prompt asks. Ads are limited to content suitable for all ages. How Google uses this information is explained at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

You can change your ad consent at any time from Privacy choices in the game's settings. You can stop tracking for every app in your iPhone's Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking. Buying Rain Check+ removes the banner and the ads between levels; ads you choose to watch for a reward are always optional.

Purchases

Rain Check+ is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and we never see your details. We only learn from Apple, on your iPhone, whether Rain Check+ is unlocked.

Children

Rain Check is a game for all ages, but it isn't made specifically for children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions about privacy: paulmessenger@hotmail.com.
