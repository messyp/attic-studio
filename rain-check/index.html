Common questions

New to it? Tap the ? on the home screen or in a level for How to play, a short walkthrough of every rule on a tiny board. You can open it again any time.

No sound? Rain Check follows your iPhone's Silent switch, so it plays quietly when your phone is on Silent. You can also mute it with the speaker button on the home screen.

Bought Rain Check+ on another device? Open Rain Check+ from the home screen and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account. Family Sharing is included.

Your progress: Levels, streaks, your Garden Diary and settings are saved on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.
