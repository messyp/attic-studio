Common questions

Controls: Tap or hold the left or right half of the screen to flip that side. Before each ball, wind the plunger with the Digital Crown and let go with a tap. A firm shake of the wrist nudges the table, but three in a few seconds is a tilt.

Can't see the flippers? The camera follows the ball and drops to the flippers as it falls. If a ball gets close to flippers that are off screen, the game slows down until they're in view.
