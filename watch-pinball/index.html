Common questions

Controls: Tap or hold the left or right half of the screen to flip that side; hold to catch the ball on a raised flipper. A flick of the Digital Crown flips both sides. Before each ball, turn the crown down to wind the plunger and let go: it fires on its own (or tap). A firm shake of the wrist nudges the table, but three in a few seconds is a tilt.

New to it? How to Play on the home screen walks you through every control on a real table in about a minute.

Can't see the flippers? The camera follows the ball and drops to the flippers as it falls. If a ball gets close to flippers that are off screen, the game slows down until they're in view.
