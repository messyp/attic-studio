Support Privacy policy

No data collected

Watch Pinball doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your watch

Your settings and high scores are kept only on your watch. The game uses the watch's motion sensors so a shake of the wrist can nudge the table. That data stays on the watch and is not recorded.
