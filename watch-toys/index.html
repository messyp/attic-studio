Common questions

What's free? Magnet, Orbs, Pop, Maze and Trace are free to play. One purchase, Unlock all toys, unlocks the rest. Each locked toy can be tried free twice, for a minute at a time.

Restoring your purchase: Open any locked toy and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. Family Sharing is included.

Haptics feel too weak or too strong: Open Settings on the home screen and choose Haptics (Off, Soft, Normal or Strong). Each toy also has its own settings behind the gear button.
