Privacy policy

Support Privacy policy

No data collected

Watch Toys doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your watch

Your settings and scores (such as bubbles popped and your best memory sequence) are kept only on your watch. Some toys use the watch's motion sensors so they can respond when you tilt or shake your wrist. That data stays on the watch and is not recorded.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions about privacy: paulmessenger@hotmail.com.
