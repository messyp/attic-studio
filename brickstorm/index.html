Common questions

Controls: Turn the Digital Crown to move the paddle, or drag a finger along the screen. Tap to serve; the ball also goes by itself after a moment. Up Moves and Crown on the home screen change which way and how fast the crown moves the paddle.

Easy, Normal or Hard: Choose under Mode on the home screen; a new climb starts in that mode. Easy lets you continue as often as you like, Normal gives you one continue, and Hard has none. A continue starts the floor again with fresh lives and your score back to zero. Climbing a whole tower on Hard wins its gold crown.

Left in the middle of a climb? Your climb is saved at the start of every floor. Open the tower again and you carry on from that floor, with the lives you had.

Your progress: Stars, crowns, best scores and climbs in progress are saved on your watch. Deleting the app deletes them.
