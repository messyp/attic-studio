Privacy policy

Support Privacy policy

No data collected

Brickstorm doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your watch

Your settings, stars, crowns, best scores and any climb in progress are kept only on your watch. The Neon tower reads the watch's time to build the clock on its first floor; that never leaves the watch.

Purchases

Anything you buy is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and we never see your details.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions about privacy: paulmessenger@hotmail.com.
