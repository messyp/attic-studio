Rewind Studio

Privacy policy

Rewind Studio is a private publishing tool. It's used only by its owner to schedule posts to a Facebook Page they manage. It isn't offered to the public and has no other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it uses the Facebook Graph API to read the owner's Page details and its queue of scheduled posts, and to schedule or cancel posts on that Page. It doesn't request or store data about anyone else.

What it doesn't do

It collects no personal data from Page visitors or followers. There's no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Data deletion

To remove the app's access, remove it in Facebook under Settings → Business integrations. Any data it holds is kept on the owner's own computer, and the owner can delete it at any time. Requests to paulmessenger@hotmail.com.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions about privacy: paulmessenger@hotmail.com.
