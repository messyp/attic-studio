Support Privacy policy

No data collected by us

Pixel MTB doesn't collect or share any personal data with us. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Your progress, scores and settings are kept only on your iPhone.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, your scores are sent to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards with your Game Center name. That's handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy, and you can turn Game Center off in your iPhone's Settings.

Changes and contact
