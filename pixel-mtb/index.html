Common questions

Controls: Hold to crouch and release to pump: let go on the back of a roller to gain speed, or right on a lip to pop higher. Drag left and right to balance, wheelie or flip in the air, and flick to grab. Tap Retry to go back to the last checkpoint.

Your progress: Stars, best scores and times are saved on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.
